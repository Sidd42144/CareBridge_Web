Cardiac Procedures Available

Coronary Artery Bypass (CABG)

Bypass surgery to restore blood flow to the heart. One of the most common heart surgeries performed in India.

  • Success Rate: 98%
  • Hospital Stay: 3-4 days
  • Cost: $8,000 - $12,000

Valve Replacement

Repair or replacement of damaged heart valves to restore normal blood flow.

  • Success Rate: 96%
  • Hospital Stay: 5-7 days
  • Cost: $10,000 - $15,000

Angioplasty & Stent

Minimally invasive procedure to open blocked arteries and improve blood flow.

  • Success Rate: 99%
  • Hospital Stay: 1-2 days
  • Cost: $5,000 - $8,000

Pacemaker Implantation

Device implantation to regulate heart rhythm and prevent arrhythmias.

  • Success Rate: 99%
  • Hospital Stay: 1-2 days
  • Cost: $4,000 - $6,000

Heart Transplant

Surgical replacement of a diseased heart with a healthy donor heart.

  • Success Rate: 90%
  • Hospital Stay: 14-21 days
  • Cost: $80,000 - $120,000

ICD (Defibrillator) Implantation

Device to monitor and correct dangerous heart rhythms automatically.

  • Success Rate: 98%
  • Hospital Stay: 1-2 days
  • Cost: $8,000 - $12,000

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does bypass surgery take?

A typical bypass surgery takes 3-4 hours. The exact time depends on how many vessels need to be bypassed.

What is the recovery time?

Most patients stay in the hospital for 3-4 days. Full recovery takes 6-8 weeks. You can resume normal activities after 2-3 months.

Is minimally invasive surgery available?

Yes, many hospitals offer minimally invasive techniques that result in smaller incisions and faster recovery.

What are the risks?

Like any surgery, there are risks. However, major cardiac surgery complications are rare with experienced surgeons. Your doctor will discuss specific risks with you.

Can I fly after surgery?

Most doctors recommend waiting 4-6 weeks before flying after cardiac surgery. We can arrange accommodation and care during this period.

Will I need follow-up care?

Yes, regular check-ups are essential. Most hospitals provide telemedicine follow-up consultations for international patients.
