Top 5 Hospitals in India for Heart Surgery

📅 Nov 10, 2025 ✏️ By CareBridge Team 🏷️ Cardiology

Heart surgery is one of the most advanced surgical procedures available today. If you're considering cardiac surgery, India offers world-class facilities at a fraction of international costs. Let's explore the top 5 hospitals in India for heart surgery.

1. Apollo Hospitals

Apollo Hospitals is the pioneer in bringing advanced cardiac care to India. With 50+ cardiologists and dedicated cardiac care units across multiple cities, Apollo has performed over 50,000 cardiac surgeries with excellent outcomes.

Specialties: CABG, Valve Replacement, Transplants, Minimally Invasive Surgery

Cost Range: $8,000 - $15,000

2. Fortis Healthcare

Fortis is known for its advanced interventional cardiology and cardiac surgery programs. Their cardiologists are trained at top international centers and use the latest technology.

Specialties: Complex CABG, Emergency Surgery, Interventional Procedures

Cost Range: $9,000 - $14,000

3. Max Healthcare

Max Healthcare has established itself as a leader in cardiac care with a team of experienced surgeons and state-of-the-art facilities.

Specialties: Robotic-Assisted Surgery, Minimally Invasive Techniques

Cost Range: $8,500 - $13,000

4. Medanta Hospital

Medanta is known for its high-volume cardiac surgery program and excellent outcomes. The hospital has performed thousands of cardiac surgeries successfully.

Specialties: Transplants, Complex Cases, Emergency Surgery

Cost Range: $9,000 - $15,000

5. Manipal Hospital

Manipal brings international standards of cardiac care with experienced surgeons and advanced technology.

Specialties: Minimally Invasive, Robotic Surgery

Cost Range: $8,000 - $12,000

Why Choose These Hospitals?

  • JCI Accreditation ensuring international standards
  • Experienced surgeons trained at leading international centers
  • Latest cardiac technology and equipment
  • High-volume programs ensuring expertise
  • Cost savings of 70-90% compared to Western countries
  • Excellent post-operative care and follow-up

Cost Comparison

A bypass surgery that costs $80,000 in the USA costs only $10,000 in India, saving you 87.5%. This makes India an attractive destination for medical tourism.
