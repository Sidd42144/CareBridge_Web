Cancer Treatment Options

Chemotherapy

Using powerful drugs to kill cancer cells throughout the body.

  • Targeted drug therapy
  • Advanced infusion techniques
  • Minimal side effect protocols

Radiation Therapy

Focused high-energy rays to target and destroy cancer cells.

  • 3D Conformal Radiation
  • IMRT (Intensity-Modulated RT)
  • Image-Guided Radiation

Surgical Oncology

Surgical removal of tumors and affected tissues.

  • Minimally invasive surgery
  • Robotic-assisted procedures
  • Reconstructive surgery

Immunotherapy

Boosting the body's immune system to fight cancer.

  • CAR-T cell therapy
  • Checkpoint inhibitors
  • Therapeutic vaccines

Targeted Therapy

Using drugs designed to target specific cancer cell weaknesses.

  • Molecular testing
  • Precision medicine
  • Personalized treatment plans

Palliative Care

Specialized care to improve quality of life and manage symptoms.

  • Pain management
  • Emotional support
  • Nutritional guidance
